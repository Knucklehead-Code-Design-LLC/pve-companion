# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the safety of its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
publication of private information without permission, and other conduct that
could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub.
Maintainers may clarify expectations, issue a warning, remove contributions or
comments, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant
when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
