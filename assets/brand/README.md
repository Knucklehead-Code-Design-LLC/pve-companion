# PVE Companion brand assets

The PVE Companion mark is an original, flat server-rack symbol. Its restrained
navy and system-blue palette keeps it legible at small sizes and lets health
states remain the only semantic colors in the product. It does not reproduce
the Proxmox wordmark or orange X symbol.

- `pve_companion_mark.svg` is the editable 1254 × 1254 source.
- `pve_companion_launch_mark.svg` is its transparent launch-screen variant.
- `pve_companion_app_icon_master.png` is the raster master rendered from it.
- `pve_companion_mark.png` is the 256 × 256 in-app mark.
- Platform app-icon renditions are rendered from the same source under the iOS
  and macOS asset catalogs.
- The iOS launch image uses the transparent variant against the app's black
  loading surface, avoiding a flash of a different background.

The mark is included under the repository's Apache-2.0 license. Do not replace
it with third-party logo artwork unless the source and license are documented
here.
